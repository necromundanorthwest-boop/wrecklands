  1. Win: collect 3 Scrap and reach your own marked extraction edge, or reduce enemy Integrity to zero.

Field guide

Choose a maneuver, then one legal action. Speed may change one step before movement with an available Driver. Risk 6+ causes Loss of Control. Contact alone causes no impact. Boarder follow-up actions unlock on a later activation begun aboard the enemy.

Ram · Impact

Contact alone causes no impact. Choose Ram explicitly at Cruise or Full Throttle when eligible; it consumes your action.

Impact = attacker Speed + D6 − defender Armor.

ImpactEffect on defender
0–1No damage; push 1 cell if legal.
2–3Lose 1 Integrity.
4–5Lose 2 Integrity and rotate 90° away; a tied left/right rotation uses one server roll.
6+Lose 3 Integrity and gain 1 Risk.

Push away from the attacker; a diagonal tie uses the attacker's facing axis. If the push cell is illegal, remain in place and gain 1 Risk.

Loss of Control · Risk 6+

At Risk ≥ 6, immediately resolve one server D6 and its effect. Then Risk resets to 3. Effects in this resolution do not recursively trigger another Loss of Control.
